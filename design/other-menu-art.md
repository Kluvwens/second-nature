# The Other Menu — v0.5 art

Nine new illustrations generated with the built-in `image_gen` tool. Final assets are copied into the project and built into `dist/assets`; no runtime dependency on the generation directory. All characters are adults. Mara is 26. The visual direction remains clean anime cel shading, expressive linework, restrained airbrushed textures, and warm dark cafe interiors.

| Form | Transformation sequence | Working scene |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Porcelain automaton | [robot-sequence.png](../assets/scenes/robot-sequence.png) | [robot-service.png](../assets/scenes/robot-service.png) |
| Porcelain cup | [cup-sequence.png](../assets/scenes/cup-sequence.png) | [regulars](../assets/scenes/cup-service.png) / [Theo](../assets/scenes/cup-service-theo.png) |
| Espresso machine | [espresso-sequence.png](../assets/scenes/espresso-sequence.png) | [espresso-service.png](../assets/scenes/espresso-service.png) |
| Paper moth | [moth-sequence.png](../assets/scenes/moth-sequence.png) | Sequence reused |
| Living shadow | [shadow-sequence.png](../assets/scenes/shadow-sequence.png) | Sequence reused |

Each sequence is a three-panel illustration: onset, an intermediate transformation, and a complete form. The cup, machine, and moth finish as actual objects/creatures without a humanoid body. Identity markers carry through in freckles, grey-green eye motifs, chestnut coloration, green/charcoal clothing-derived materials, and Mara's brass clip.

The sequences use [cafe-welcome.png](../assets/scenes/cafe-welcome.png) as a character/style reference. Service scenes use the matching transformation sequence as the final-form design reference. Those scenes show the espresso machine brewing and steaming through service, the cup being used for tea by an adult regular, and the robot performing precise work while questioning her automatic routines.

Exact prompts and reference names: [transformation prompt set](other-menu-art-prompts.json), [service prompt set](other-menu-service-prompts.json), [alternate cup-service prompt](cup-service-theo-prompt.txt). The alternate cup scene edits the regulars illustration to show Theo on the familiar-hands branch. It is saved separately, with the original retained. The game selects the correct service image and remembers each experienced variant in the gallery.

All originals remain unmodified. The game retains the established View illustration dialog and Open full-resolution image hyperlink. Discovered sequences are retained after recovery; service images enter the gallery after the work-session choice. The new gallery is available through Mara and at the chapter ending.
