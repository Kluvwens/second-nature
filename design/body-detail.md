# Version 0.9 — a minute to yourself

The five later forms each have an optional pause before work. Mara can try an old habit or investigate a new sensation. The ten outcomes give her time to be frightened, curious, irritated, or pleased without making any of those reactions settle the question for her. Returning from the experiment shows a short handoff to the work choices, so the player does not have to read the adjustment scene twice.

The experiment adds one notebook entry per shift. It changes no wages, relationships, exposure count, work limits, or recovery time. Its result survives saves and cannot be applied twice. Earlier saves need no migration; the optional field is absent until the player takes the new choice. The established story and save identifiers are unchanged.

The expansion also adds physical detail to the five change sequences, cup service, all five nights and recoveries, and the original honey evening. Ordinary scenes continue using the compact reader. Transformation passages retain their longer scrolling format. All new game prose uses the write-naturally skill.
