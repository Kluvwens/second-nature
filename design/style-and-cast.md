# Second Nature — style and cast proposal

Status: first-pass proposal, updated 2026-09-19. The user selected simpler clean cel-shaded anime illustration with airbrushed textures and expressive linework. Working title, names, supporting cast, housing arrangement, and world rules remain open for revision.

## Established by the user

- The protagonist is a woman in her mid twenties.
- She starts working at a strange coffee shop or cafe.
- The drinks transform her.
- She has a compelling reason to keep working there; the exact reason is still open.
- She notices the changes and struggles with enjoying them.
- Play centers on work, relationships, and managing transformations.
- Transformations should receive detailed written and visual treatment.
- Transformations can be extreme and supernatural: the first requested form is a honey slime woman, replacing the earlier small hair-color change.
- Include a scene of Mara dropping off her belongings in her new apartment before work.
- Mara should be more frightened even when some sensations feel good. Awareness and curiosity do not make immediate acceptance plausible.
- Regular scenes should need little scrolling, more moments should be illustrated, and the page theme should be darker and warmer.
- The specials are the cafe's secret attraction. Mara's first transformation wears off overnight; later transformations may take longer to fade.
- The illustration style should be anime influenced, with simpler clean cel shading, airbrushed textures, and expressive linework.
- Use the write-naturally skill for all game writing and revisions. The standing instruction and skill location are in [AGENTS.md](../AGENTS.md).

## Core direction

A contemporary neighborhood cafe that feels like somewhere you could become a regular. Morning queues, closing chores, missed buses, and familiar orders give the strange drinks a believable setting.

The proposed emotional center: **She can enjoy a sensation while being frightened by the change.** Her first responses include denial, checking her body, asking for the change to stop, and pressing for reversal. Pleasure interrupts that fear rather than resolving it. Curiosity, anger, discomfort, and attachment can coexist; emotional recovery takes longer than learning a useful physical trick.

Working title: **Second Nature**, also the cafe's name.

Proposed reason to stay: the position comes with an affordable studio above the cafe. Mara has lost her previous job, her shared lease has ended, and her savings are thin. She wants enough money for a deposit on her own place. Leaving immediately would cost her an income and the only housing she can presently afford. Over time, friendship, pride in her work, curiosity, and enjoyment complicate this initially practical arrangement.

The housing arrangement is ordinary and disclosed upfront. Drinking experimental specials is a separate decision. Financial progress should be real, so the initial reason to stay can evolve instead of becoming an endlessly moving target.

## Visual language

**Simple cel-shaded anime illustration with airbrushed textures and expressive linework.** Strong silhouettes, flat local colors, and one principal clean-edged shadow tone per material. Use a few restrained highlights and smooth airbrushed color transitions inside larger shapes, particularly on cheeks, hair, and softly lit clothing. Keep cel-shadow boundaries crisp.

Linework carries the acting: confident thicker outer contours, thin selective interior lines, lively tapered curves, and expressive eyebrows, eyes, mouths, and hands. Faces remain visibly adult with distinct age cues and natural adult proportions.

Group hair into large designed locks. Simplify braids into readable grouped shapes and clothing into a few meaningful folds. Avoid individual-strand rendering, fabric weave, dense crosshatching, and incidental microdetail. Preserve each character's identity anchors and everyday clothing. The finish should be clean and softly airbrushed, without canvas grain or gritty texture.

Warm interiors against rainy blue streets. Espresso brown, cream, tarnished copper, deep jade, and muted plum. The cafe has old green tile, dark wooden counters, warm lamps, and slightly mismatched ceramics. Simplify the environment into broad architectural and prop shapes with restrained airbrushed atmosphere. Keep background detail subordinate to the characters.

Show the supernatural change clearly, including material, silhouette, movement, and practical consequences. Expressions and reflections carry Mara's uncertainty even when the physical transformation is unmistakable. Skin, hair, fabric, posture, and objects should remain readable.

Characters look distinctive at baseline. Their initial designs must be worth returning to; transformation is not a correction of an intentionally unattractive starting point.

Presentation: dark espresso and walnut panels, warm cream text, copper accents, and amber illustration lighting. A compact top bar and status strip leave room for the scene. The illustration sits beside the reading panel on wide screens and above it on narrow screens. Regular scenes paginate to their available space, with Back/Continue controls and choices on the last page. Detailed transformation scenes can scroll. Character, relationship, and notebook detail lives in dialogs. Every scene has art, with human/honey variants where the branch requires them.

## Writing voice

Close second person in present tense. Mara has an identifiable dry, observant voice; the player chooses how she responds.

Use concrete details and short internal reactions. Each transformation beat should establish:
1. What she observes.
2. How she checks whether it is real.
3. The practical or social consequence.
4. Her mixed interpretation.
5. What she chooses to do next.

Sample tone:

> You have always had to repeat yourself over the grinder.
>
> This time, Theo turns before you finish the order. Your voice carried cleanly across the counter: lower, steadier, with none of the uncertain lift you usually hear at the end.
>
> You try the next order quietly. Same result.
>
> It could be practice. Except practice does not explain why the voice memo from yesterday sounds like someone doing an impression of you.
>
> You play it again in the stockroom.
>
> Then you say the same sentence aloud.
>
> You like today's version. That is the part you keep coming back to.

Keep the player's awareness intact. Repeated descriptions and dialogue can show changing interpretation, while important changes remain recorded in the journal.

## Core cast

### Mara Ellis — 26 — protagonist

**Baseline appearance:** light olive skin, freckles across a gently crooked nose, gray-green eyes, shoulder-length wavy chestnut hair often tied half back. Medium everyday build. Faded green shirts, dark jeans, practical shoes, a charcoal waist apron, and an old brass hair clip.

**Personality:** observant, dryly funny, competent in a rush, uncomfortable asking directly for things. She knows how to make herself useful and sometimes mistakes that for knowing what she wants.

**Immediate want:** steady work, a room with a closing door, and enough savings to choose her next move.

**Private fear:** that liking a change means she was dishonest about who she was before.

**Character tension:** she is aware of what is happening. Confidence, ease, and attention can feel good without answering whether the changes are right for her.

**Visual continuity:** face structure, distinctive nose, freckle pattern, and brass clip are useful identity anchors. Any route that changes an anchor must do so deliberately and record it.

### Inez Vale — 42 — owner

**Appearance:** warm brown skin, angular mature face with fine smile lines, a chin-length dark bob with one silver streak. Plum blouse, charcoal apron, small brass earrings. Economical, unhurried movements.

**Personality:** considerate, exacting, quietly funny. Remembers people's orders and notices evasions. Explains practical details readily but becomes guarded about the cafe's history.

**Want:** keep the cafe and the community around it alive.

**Blind spot:** believes a change that helped her will probably help someone else.

**Relationship role:** employer and possible mentor. Trust grows when she gives Mara useful, checkable information and respects her decisions; her omissions can damage it.

**Long-term direction:** Inez may be preparing Mara to take over the cafe. Let this emerge through work and accumulated trust, well before anyone names succession. Version 0.6 plants only early hints: checking an invoice, noticing a regular's needs, listening to a supplier call, making a stock estimate, and being allowed to arrange the counter. Inez asks for Mara's judgment before giving her own. These moments also happen on ordinary-coffee shifts; changing form is not a prerequisite for being trusted with the shop.

Do not turn the five-shift trial into an ownership offer, a transfer of keys, or a declaration that Mara is the chosen successor. The staying ending offers to sit in on a future shop order. Inez's history with the previous owner gets one passing mention. Her reasons, the business arrangement, Mara's eventual wishes, and Theo's existing hope of part-ownership are later story questions, not resolved here.

**Open mystery:** how much she knows, what changed her, and whether she understands the current drinks completely. Do not lock her into an omniscient villain.

### Theo Park — 29 — senior barista

**Appearance:** East Asian man, broad sturdy build, short untidy black hair, square dark glasses, small silver ear stud. Clay-brown overshirt, oatmeal tee, green work apron.

**Personality:** patient when teaching, playful under pressure, less relaxed when conversation becomes personal. Takes coffee and staff breaks seriously.

**Want:** become part-owner rather than remain indispensable without having a say.

**Blind spot:** normalizes odd things because the job has been good to him.

**Relationship role:** coworker, practical ally, and a person with his own history of change. He can help Mara learn a drink's pattern, disagree with Inez, and still withhold an embarrassing part of his own experience.

**Friction:** he may interpret Mara's questions about the cafe as judgment of choices he made.

### Nadia Bell — 27 — longtime friend

**Appearance:** Black woman with dark brown skin, copper-tipped braids in a loose bun, expressive brows. Navy medical scrubs after her radiography shifts, often under a camel coat.

**Personality:** affectionate, perceptive, direct, sometimes too quick to offer a solution.

**Want:** maintain their friendship as their schedules and circumstances change.

**Blind spot:** assumes that knowing Mara's past gives her a reliable picture of Mara's present wishes.

**Relationship role:** a connection outside the cafe with independent work, plans, and needs. She can notice changes the staff find ordinary.

**Friction:** concern can become possessiveness about the familiar version of a friend. Her story is learning when to question, when to listen, and how to make room for Mara's choices.

Nadia is not acclimated to the cafe's transformations. Recognition should take time: she looks for Mara's missing body, reaches and recoils, asks overlapping questions, and needs a familiar voice or memory. Support comes through shock rather than replacing it. Even after they begin talking, her attention returns to the impossible physical details. Version 0.6 tracks which new forms she has witnessed; seeing the same form again is still unsettling without making her forget the earlier encounter. Private reflections do not grant her knowledge she did not receive.

Romance preferences and routes are not yet established. Supporting characters have motives and relationships beyond any possible romance.

### After-hours discoveries

Version 0.8 gives each companion two optional recovery-evening meetings. Nadia gets a cinema outing and a difficult workday of her own. Theo shows Mara a photograph of his first transformation into a tabletop radio, then admits he wants a share in the business. Inez introduces **Bea**, an adult regular spending her day off as a wooden coat stand, and lets Mara read older records. **Ada** is the previous owner who taught Inez the recipes. Inez's name appears in the recovery book, but her first form remains undisclosed. These discoveries deepen the existing relationships without resolving succession. Choices are remembered in later shifts and the People panel. See [after-hours.md](after-hours.md) for branches and illustrations.

## Detailed transformation presentation

### Duration and the other menu

The cafe sells ordinary coffee and has an off-menu selection known to some regulars. Those customers come specifically for a temporary change and share the secret by word of mouth. A normal order remains a normal order.

For Mara, the specials are encountered in the working day, not displayed as a catalogue of bodies. Version 0.6 introduces one through a rush, a regular's quiet ritual, a machine problem, a tea delivery, or a lighting fault. The player follows the incident or stays with ordinary coffee. Drink names and sensory hints can appear; transformation outcomes are not printed on the choice buttons. All five routes remain reachable in one campaign. The encounter is saved for the visit, and older menu saves derive one deterministically without changing state while rendering.

Mara's first Golden Hour wears off overnight. The next-morning scene returns her completely to her human baseline. Relief comes first; she checks her hands, face, hair, and footing. Memories, photos, relationships, and any conflicting feelings persist. Her first observed duration is a reference point, not a guarantee for every future cup.

Version 0.5 makes slower recovery concrete across five additional shifts. The new arc counts specials already tasted, including the original honey special. A first new special after ordinary coffee lasts 18 hours; later doses last 26, 27, 28, 29, then at most 30 hours. Each dose records a starting time and recovery time; the 06:50 check can find Mara still changed. The story then observes recovery and leaves the rest of that day free before the next scheduled shift. This is a provisional authored curve, not an open-ended simulation. Enjoying a form, working longer, or trusting someone does not cause longer duration or permanence.

The implemented overnight transition is specific to the first cup. It is not a general rule that sleeping instantly cures every transformation. Later shifts should track elapsed duration and the last observed recovery separately from choosing to rest. The gallery records past discoveries even when Mara is currently human.

### Stages and continuity

Treat each drink as having a recognizable family of effects, discoverable through experience. Golden Hour gives the honey slime form. Clockwork Cream gives a porcelain automaton; Small Hours, a porcelain cup; House Pressure, an espresso machine; Margin Tea, a paper moth; Afterimage, a living shadow. Each new route includes a three-panel illustration and detailed prose for onset, complete transformation, adjustment, work, aftermath, evening, and recovery.

Track separately:
- Observable physical changes.
- Sensory or behavioral changes.
- Mara's awareness and interpretation.
- What each other character knows.
- What she has chosen to disclose.

For detailed art, first approve a baseline character reference with front, side, three-quarter, and expression views. Each selected progression then gets a short visual specification describing exactly what changes and what stays constant.

Use matched camera, pose, outfit, and lighting for comparison portraits. Detailed transformation sequences also need close views of material changes and a complete-body illustration. Major narrative moments can receive separate scene illustrations.

Maintain a continuity record linking the written stage to its artwork: hair, eyes, face, height, silhouette, posture, voice, and clothing fit as applicable. Do not silently change unrelated features between images.

Detailed transformations should remain legible within the simpler art style: concentrate visual detail on the feature that is changing, with expressive reactions and matched comparison views. Keep unrelated hair, fabric, and background rendering simple across stages.

Golden Hour's implemented progression:

1. **Baseline:** Mara carries her bags into her apartment, sees her familiar reflection, and chooses whether to leave her blue mug out.
2. **Hands and forearms:** skin becomes translucent amber; fingers flatten under pressure and pull into connected honey threads. A close-up shows light passing through the changing material.
3. **Face and hair:** the mirror reveals amber spreading across her face and chestnut locks turning into honey ribbons. A cheek indentation and still-human patches make the intermediate stage readable.
4. **Complete honey form:** her whole body, hair, and feet are living amber honey. Her feet soften into a connected pool; her opaque work clothing remains fabric. The full-body scene shows both her altered silhouette and her effort to hold it together.
5. **Control and interpretation:** the player practices a familiar shape or explores flexibility, then handles an order and other people's reactions. The closing scene revisits the same apartment and mirror.
6. **The following morning:** she wakes human and checks her familiar body with relief. A new apartment illustration records the aftermath. Her first recovery is added to the notebook, while the honey artwork stays unlocked.

Freckles, gray-green eyes, face structure, and brass clip maintain identity. Her feelings do not determine whether the transformation occurred. Other characters visibly notice her complete new form; withholding an explanation cannot conceal it.

Each illustrated stage opens in a larger view with a full-resolution link. The sidebar reflects the current stage, and the gallery only reveals stages already experienced. The matched portrait comparison becomes available after the complete change.

The first honey progression established the visual language. The expanded chapter offers five distinct forms without combinatorial hybrids. Cup, machine, and robot also receive separate service illustrations. The current gallery retains forms across recovery, with illustration access separated from the current physical state.

## Current prototype

Implemented as version 0.8 in `dist/index.html`; editable prose is in `src/story/first-shift.twee`, `other-menu.twee`, `new-transformations.twee`, and `after-hours.twee`. The honey form, overnight first recovery, secret specials, five new forms, later recovery, natural discovery, and early mentorship hints follow the user's selected direction. Version 0.7 applied the write-naturally editorial pass; version 0.8 adds optional evenings and relationship consequences. Exact timing and dialogue remain working examples. See [validation notes](prototype-validation.md).

One apartment move-in, the first shift with three orders and optional honey special, a next-morning epilogue, then five further shifts with five new specials or ordinary coffee. New routes offer two approaches and two session lengths; conversations with Nadia or private accounts follow. Repeated drinks persist into recovery days. The five-shift trial ends with staying on written terms or taking Nadia's temporary spare room. Includes retained discoveries, journals, relationships, earnings, and saving. All five new transformations can be experienced in one campaign.

The prototype should demonstrate that a drink affects the workday, that Mara can notice and question it, and that another person can react meaningfully.
