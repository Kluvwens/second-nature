# Example: Hex & Honey

The user rejected a second presentation pass as still looking AI-coded. The earlier revision had changed colors, typography, and borders. It added useful game mechanics but retained a three-column panel layout, a large logo in an empty board center, repeated moth icons, and rules rendered in small type. The lesson is about diagnosis, not about banning these individual visual treatments.

## Credited comparison

Reviewed 4 October 2026:

- [Root, official component gallery and credits](https://ledergames.com/products/root-a-game-of-woodland-might-and-right). Game design: Cole Wehrle. Illustration: Kyle Ferrin. Graphic design and layout: Cole Wehrle, Kyle Ferrin, Nick Brachmann, and Jaime Willems. Visually inspected the fall-board photograph and Eyrie player-board photograph.
- [Dungeon Degenerates, publisher component gallery](https://goblinkomegamall.com/pages/retailers). The publisher credits Sean Äaberg as creator and illustrator. Visually inspected its map image. The art's varied terrain and building silhouettes distinguish places before reading rules.

| Observed reference | Defect in the prototype | Design response |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Root's light paths and clearings stand out against the forest; pieces occupy the map. | The playable area is an even border of icon buttons surrounding repeated branding. | Make the board a place. Draw the readable route and state over an original café map; keep the same spaces and game rules. |
| Root's faction sheet groups actions and tracks according to what players do. | Five empty body slots and long panels have similar weight to rolling and choosing. | Group the dice, destination, and action together. Make the character record compact; reserve the main reading surface for the current decision. |
| Dungeon Degenerates uses specific buildings and terrain instead of one repeated generic emblem. | A moth icon stands in for many different scenes. | Give the market, fountain, mirror, salon, and exit identifiable silhouettes. Use original art, not copied reference imagery. |

The target is an illustrated café board with separate, readable controls and an encounter page. Do not describe generated art as the credited artists' work, or call the result human-made. Do not import Dungeon Degenerates' gore, palette, or characters into the unrelated café brief.

Verify a fresh game, a transformation offer, and a populated combination state. On a narrow screen, check the main action and reading size, not only whether the page technically fits. Compare before and after at the same width. The engine's save and probability tests remain necessary, but they do not validate the visual judgment.
