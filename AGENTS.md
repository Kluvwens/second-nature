# Game writing

Use the **write-naturally** skill whenever writing or revising text for this game. Resolve it from the available skills in your environment, read it before the first writing pass in a task, and apply it throughout. This is the user's standing preference, including dialogue, narration, choice labels, notebook entries, and other player-facing copy.

Preserve the game's second-person, present-tense voice and the character direction in `design/style-and-cast.md`. Prefer specific actions and thoughts to explanations of what a scene means. Let dialogue sound like the particular person speaking. Keep effective imagery, detailed transformation sensations, and Mara's conflicting reactions; natural writing does not mean flattening or shortening every passage.

For editorial changes, preserve choices, conditions, state transitions, and save compatibility. Update copy-sensitive checks when wording changes, then build and run the existing tests. Do not change story events or character arcs just to improve a sentence.
